My Reflection in SAD

I learned that System Analysis and Design (SAD) helps us understand how systems work, analyze problems, and find effective solutions. As a student, it helps me improve my critical thinking and teaches me to carefully analyze situations before making decisions. In real life, I can use what I learned to solve problems, organize tasks, and make better choices. I believe SAD will help me in my studies and prepare me for my future job by teaching me how to analyze problems and create simple and practical solutions.

My Reflection on DFD and Structured Analysis

I learned that a Data Flow Diagram (DFD) is useful for showing how information moves within a system. It helps me understand the relationship between processes, data stores, external entities, and data flows. I also learned that structured analysis makes it easier to study a system by dividing it into smaller parts. These topics taught me the importance of organizing information and understanding each process before developing a system. I can apply this knowledge to school projects and future work by creating clear diagrams and identifying how information should move from one part of a system to another.

My Reflection on Logic Models and Decision Tools

I learned that logic models, Structured English, decision tables, and decision trees help organize information and make decisions more clearly. A logic model shows the relationship between resources, activities, outputs, and expected outcomes. Structured English explains a process using simple words and step-by-step instructions. A decision table presents different conditions and their corresponding actions, while a decision tree uses branches to show possible choices and results. These lessons helped me understand how to solve problems in an organized way. I believe these tools will be useful in my studies and future career because they can help me analyze situations, understand processes, and make appropriate decisions.
